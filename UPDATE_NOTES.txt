- The mouse wheel no longer stops working after it was first used while a blocked mod key was held
- A mod's blocked keys are released when the mod is unloaded or reloaded, instead of staying blocked
- Mod windows no longer flicker while a save loads, and blink less after Alt-Tab
- After a graphics crash (the game losing its graphics device), the mod windows now come back by themselves: they stay off for one launch as a precaution, and the log says so. Before, they stayed off until you edited modloader.ini. If your mod windows are still off from an earlier version, set Enabled=1 under [UI] in ModLoader\modloader.ini
- The log now records why the graphics device was lost and which step of a game icon copy failed, to help find the cause of a problem
- Mod overlay buttons can now be clicked while the build menu is open
